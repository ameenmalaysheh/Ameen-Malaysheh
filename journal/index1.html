About the Journal

The MLRI Journal for Interdisciplinary Research provides an organized archive of research associated with the intellectual and research vision of the Metaphysical Light Research Institute.

Its areas of interest include human self and mind, consciousness, economic models, archaeology, engineering, philosophy, and interdisciplinary research.
