About the Journal

The MLRI Journal for Interdisciplinary Research provides an organized archive of research associated with the intellectual and research vision of the Metaphysical Light Research Institute.

Its areas of interest include human self and mind, consciousness, economic models, archaeology, engineering, philosophy, and interdisciplinary research.

Aims & Scope | الأهداف والنطاق العلمي

The journal publishes interdisciplinary research and theoretical frameworks in the following areas:

  • Human self-structure and philosophy of mind.
  • The Malaysheh clay-mold theory in historical engineering and ancient-civilization architecture.
  • The Nations’ Barter Bank framework, barter systems, and alternative economic models.
  • Metaphysical and interdisciplinary studies.
